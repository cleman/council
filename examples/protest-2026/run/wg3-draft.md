## 1. Removal/residence risk — four distinct regimes (corrects "foreign nationals" lumped together)
- **(a) EU/EEA/Swiss citizens.** No residence permit to withdraw and the 2024 "republican-principles contract" does not apply. Removal (OQTF or expulsion) requires that *personal conduct* be a "menace réelle, actuelle et suffisamment grave pour un intérêt fondamental de la société" (CESEDA L251-1 for OQTF; same test for expulsion). A conviction alone is not enough; after 10 years' lawful residence only "imperative" public-security grounds count. A protest arrest without serious conviction is very unlikely to meet this test. [VERIFIED for the rule; CREDIBLE for low risk] — conseil-etat.fr (CRP 507674, Feb 2026); village-justice.com/articles/eloignement-citoyen-europeen…,52953
- **(b) Non-EU residents with a titre de séjour.** Temporary/multi-year cards can be withdrawn for "menace à l'ordre public"; 10-year carte de résident only for a *serious* threat (CESEDA L432-4 ff.). The 2024 immigration law (loi 2024-42, art. 46) added a contract to respect republican principles: serious breach can mean refusal/withdrawal; it also weakened protections against expulsion after convictions for offences punishable by several years. Decisions must be reasoned and are appealable; arrest alone is not automatic grounds, but the prefecture can consult police files (see TAJ). Still the highest-stakes adult group. [VERIFIED law; CREDIBLE on practice] — legifrance.gouv.fr/jorf/article_jo/JORFARTI000049040337 ; gisti.org (ce que change la loi 2024) ; CNCDH opinion A-2024-7
- **(c) Asylum seekers / refugees.** Arrest does not suspend the claim or cancel the attestation, but a prefect can switch the claim to the *accelerated procedure* for "menace grave" (CESEDA L531-27), or seek house arrest/OQTF. Ending refugee status for public-order reasons requires a final conviction for a very serious offence (terrorism or ≥10-year offence) — far beyond typical protest charges. [CREDIBLE; mainly law-firm sources] — kohenavocats.com/demandeur-asile-interpelle-manifestation… ; bpda.assas-universite.fr (OFPRA & ordre public)
- **(d) Dual nationals (French + other).** As French citizens they cannot be removed. Loss of nationality (déchéance) applies only to naturalised persons, for terrorism or attacks on fundamental national interests — not protest offences. Only ordinary criminal/TAJ consequences. [CREDIBLE, Code civil art. 25; no protest-specific case found]

## 2. TAJ (police records file)
An arrest is recorded even without prosecution. After a dismissal (classement sans suite), data are **not automatically deleted**; they are flagged ("mention") so they are hidden from *administrative vetting* — but the flag is often not added automatically and must be requested. Administrative vetting covers private security, airport/port restricted zones, police/justice/sensitive public posts, some childcare roles and naturalisation/residence. Deletion request: registered letter to the **prosecutor of the place of the investigation**; appeal to the president of the chambre de l'instruction; access via the Interior Ministry / CNIL (indirect access). [VERIFIED core (CNIL; CPP art. 230-8)] — cnil.fr/fr/les-refus-dembauche-un-poste-dagent-de-securite… ; village-justice.com/articles/fichier-traitement-des-antecedents-judiciaires-taj-comment-effacer,30921

## 3. Phone in custody (GAV)
- Police take the phone during GAV. **Searching its content**: CJEU 4 Oct 2024 (C-548/21) requires prior review by a court/independent authority except in urgency. **But** Cass. crim. 19 May 2026 (n°25-87.563) and 10 Mar 2026 hold that signing written consent to a search of personal effects also counts as consent to phone exploitation. Practical point: do not sign a consent form without a lawyer. [CREDIBLE; read via law blogs only] — baudelinavocat.fr (CJUE) ; kohenavocats.com/consentement-fouille-exploitation…-19-mai-2026
- **Unlock code**: Cass. Ass. plén. 7 Nov 2022 (n°21-83.146) — refusal can be an offence (Code pénal 434-15-2: up to 3 yrs/€270k) **only if** the phone has active encryption the code unlocks, after a formal request by an authority. Modern smartphones usually meet this. Prosecutions of protesters on this basis: frequency UNKNOWN. [VERIFIED] — service-public.gouv.fr/particuliers/actualites/A16210
- LDH advice: leave the phone at home or use a spare; memorise a lawyer/relative's number.

## 4. Kettling (nasse)
Conseil d'État 10 June 2021 (n°444849, LDH & SNJ) annulled the encirclement provision of the 2020 national policing doctrine (SNMO) because it did not define when it may be used; also annulled limits on journalists. Kettling is not banned outright: it must be justified, limited in time, with an exit offered. LDH's Aug 2026 rights sheet: systematic ID checks on leaving a kettle are unlawful; don't resist physically, document and complain later. [VERIFIED] — village-justice.com/articles/accreditation-des-journalistes…,39533 ; ldh-france.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/Fiche-triptique-A4_Nosdroits_manif_Aout-2026.pdf

## 6. Low-exposure alternatives — evidence

## 7. Public servants
Free to march off duty as private citizens (no rule forbids it). Neutrality binds on duty; the devoir de réserve is case-law, applies off duty too, and is stricter for senior/police/judicial posts. Teachers (State staff): 5-day union notice; primary teachers must declare intent to their IEN 48h ahead (incl. one working day) → for Thu 5 Nov, by Mon 2 Nov; pay docked **1/30 of the month per day, indivisible** even for partial-day absence. Childcare service by the commune if >25% strike. [CREDIBLE; union/law-firm sources] — se-unsa.org/2025/11/que-faire-lorsque-je-souhaite-faire-greve-3 ; 75.snuipp.fr (devoir de réserve)

## 8. Minors
GAV possible from 13 (10–13: "retenue" max 12h+12h, only for ≥5-year offences); lawyer **mandatory** from the start; parents must be informed. Parents are civilly liable for damage their child causes (Code civil 1242), not criminally for joining a protest. Injuries: Education Minister cited **190 pupils injured** since the movement began (early Oct; corrects "no official count") — incl. a 15-year-old who lost a hand to a grenade in Lens, 5 Oct (IGPN inquiry; circumstances disputed). [CREDIBLE] — franceinfo.fr (…_8223883) ; justifit.fr guide GAV mineurs

## Glossary
- **CESEDA** – French code on foreigners' entry/residence/asylum.
- **OQTF** – order to leave French territory.
- **Titre de séjour** – residence permit; *carte de résident* = 10-year card.
- **OFPRA / CNDA** – asylum office / asylum appeals court.
- **GAV (garde à vue)** – police custody (24h, extendable).
- **TAJ** – national police file of people involved in investigations (suspects and victims).
- **CNIL** – data-protection authority.
- **CJEU (CJUE)** – EU Court of Justice.
- **Nasse** – kettling: police encircling a crowd.
- **SNMO** – national public-order policing doctrine.
- **LDH** – Human Rights League (legal observers, guides).
- **IGPN** – police internal-affairs inspectorate.
- **IEN** – primary-school inspector (teachers' line manager).
- **Classement sans suite** – prosecutor drops the case.
- **Devoir de réserve** – civil servants' duty of restraint in public expression.
