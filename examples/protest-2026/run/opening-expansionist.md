# Opening position: Expansionist

## 1. Position
If you share a strand's demands, take part, and choose leverage over visibility. That means the daytime main body of a declared march, or the **Thu 5 Nov** joint union strike (intersyndicale) [VERIFIED, WG2 §2c]. Leave before the march disperses. Non-EU residence-permit holders, and anyone with a grievance about 17 Oct, get more from testifying than from marching (point 3). Staying home has a cost too, because the window in point 1 closes within weeks.

## 2. Strongest arguments
**1. Decisions are due now.** Prime Minister Lecornu ordered decisions "before the end of October" [VERIFIED, WG2 §4]. The Assemblée debates spending 27 Oct–16 Nov and votes the 2027 budget on 17 Nov [CREDIBLE, WG2 §1]. The movement has already won about 3,000 redeployed substitute teachers and +€1.2bn for education [VERIFIED as announcements, WG2 §4]. Unions call this inadequate, which shows the government responds, not that it has yielded. Late-October turnout counts more than turnout in December.

**2. Peaceful turnout has a measured effect.** Madestam et al. (QJE 2013) used rain on rally days as a natural experiment. Bigger rallies produced lasting movement strength and votes, more than one extra vote per protester ([paper](https://dash.harvard.edu/entities/publication/73120378-e077-6bd4-e053-0100007fdf3b)) [VERIFIED as published; US]. Wasow (APSR 2020) finds nonviolent protest moved opinion and votes, while violence pushed coverage toward "social control" ([APSR](https://econpapers.repec.org/RePEc:cup:apsrev:v:114:y:2020:i:3:p:638-659_3)) [VERIFIED as published; effect sizes vary by version]. There is no French evidence. Calm marches in mid-sized cities carry the most value and the least risk [CREDIBLE, WG1 §5].

**3. Witnesses now have a formal channel.** The Assemblée inquiry can compel witnesses and take sworn testimony [VERIFIED, WG1 §2]. Both deaths are unexplained and nobody has counted injured protesters [UNKNOWN, WG1 §3–4]. Dated, located footage can enter the official record without you joining a crowd.
