# Fact Desk 3: arrest totals, budget votes, turnout, 24 Oct (as of 20 Oct 2026)
Method: 12 WebSearch "extended" queries. WebFetch is broken, so every point here rests on search summaries, not on the full articles. Nothing dated after 20 Oct can be known.

## 1. The 17 Oct arrest totals, reconciled
All the figures are **successive provisional tallies of the same event**: Saturday 17 Oct plus the night that followed. They do not cover different scopes, and no source breaks the totals down by night.
- **Sat 17 Oct, evening:** PM Lecornu gave **~1,200 arrests and 150 police/gendarmes injured**. VERIFIED ([BFM on X](https://x.com/BFMTV/status/2111580393816130024), [20 Minutes relay](https://www.shango.media/20-minutes-mobilisation-du-17-octobre-1-200-interpellations-150-policiers-blesses-lecornu-appelle-a-choisir-son-camp-11958236)).
- **Sun 18 Oct, morning:** Interior Minister Nuñez posted **~1,800 arrests and >500 police, gendarmes and firefighters injured** on X, calling it "provisional". VERIFIED ([ICI](https://www.ici.fr/infos/faits-divers-justice/mobilisation-du-17-octobre-1-800-interpellations-en-france-indique-le-ministre-de-l-interieur-laurent-nunez-6459330), [actu17](https://actu17.fr/faits-divers/mobilisation-du-17-octobre-1-800-interpellations-et-plus-de-500-policiers-gendarmes-et-pompiers-blesses.html)). **Correction to Fact Desk 2:** the 1,800 came from Nuñez, not the PM.
- **Sun 18 Oct, midday:** Nuñez gave >2,000 arrests and >600 injured. CREDIBLE ([Pandore](https://www.pandore-gendarmerie.org/violences-17-octobre-2026-chronologie-bilan-forces-secours/)).
- **Sun 18 Oct, about 21:43:** the Interior Ministry gave **2,397 arrests and 997 police and gendarmes injured, 7 of them seriously**. VERIFIED as the latest official figure ([CNEWS live 18 Oct](https://www.cnews.fr/france/2026-10-18/mobilisation-du-17-octobre-2397-personnes-ont-ete-interpellees-et-997-policiers), [Atlasinfo](https://atlasinfo.fr/2-397-interpellations-en-france-lors-des-manifestations-contre-la-vie-chere.html)). The 21:43 timestamp comes from a secondary site (Pandore). The ministry itself called it provisional ([CNEWS 19 Oct](https://www.cnews.fr/france/2026-10-19/mobilisation-du-17-octobre-apres-les-saccages-et-pillages-laurent-nunez-attendu)), and one relay adds firefighters to the 997.
- **Best statement:** "By the evening of 18 Oct, the Interior Ministry's provisional count for 17 Oct and the following night was 2,397 arrests nationally (855 in Paris) and 997 police and gendarmes injured (7 seriously). Earlier figures of 1,200 (PM, 17 Oct) and 1,800 (Nuñez, 18 Oct) were partial counts that this one supersedes." Fact Desk 2's "CONTRADICTED" label for 2,397 should be dropped.

## 3. PLF 2026 Part 1, autumn 2025
VERIFIED. The Assemblée rejected Part 1 on **21 Nov 2025 by 1 for, 404 against and 84 abstentions**. The single vote for came from Harold Huwart (LIOT). The whole bill was therefore rejected at first reading and went to the Sénat. It was finally passed under 49.3 on 2 Feb 2026 ([Assemblée nationale](https://www.assemblee-nationale.fr/dyn/actualites-accueil-hub/projet-de-loi-de-finances-pour-2026-rejet-en-premiere-lecture), [LCP](https://lcp.fr/actualites/budget-2026-l-assemblee-nationale-rejette-la-partie-recettes-le-texte-va-etre-transmis), [Legifiscal](https://www.legifiscal.fr/actualites-fiscales/4341-vote-quasi-unanimite-deputes-contre-plf-2026.html)). **Caveat:** these rates are upper bounds for a peaceful marcher. The arrests include looters who were not part of the marches: the préfectures count 1,500–2,000 such people in Toulouse and about 400 in Grenoble. Turnout counts also differ by a factor of 2 to 14 from union counts.
