## 3. Where and when violence concentrated on 17 Oct (correction)
The prior brief placed risk mainly in evening and wildcat actions. **The evidence now shows violence often began at assembly points in the early afternoon and carried on into the evening.**
- Paris: a car burned at République at 14:10, a kiosk at 15:11, vehicles from 16:07, looting on bd Magenta through the afternoon, and police vehicles attacked around 20:00. CREDIBLE (Actu17 live feed: https://actu17.fr/faits-divers/en-direct-mobilisation-du-17-octobre-suivez-les-evenements-partout-en-france.html).
- Lyon: tear gas at Bellecour from the 14:00 rally start, and the tourist-office kiosk still burning at 15:00. CREDIBLE (https://www.letribunaldunet.fr/faits-divers/lyon-office-tourisme-bellecour-incendie-manifestation.html).
- Lille: clashes from about 12:30, before the march set off, lasting into the late evening (préfet, via Wikipedia). CREDIBLE.
- Strasbourg and Mulhouse: the cortèges marched calmly and violence broke out "en marge" (on the edges). CREDIBLE.
- Limoges: arson around 18:00. Rennes: peaceful until late afternoon (from the prior brief).
- **Pattern:** two zones carried the risk. One was large city-centre gathering points in the biggest cities (Paris, Lyon, Lille, Marseille, Bordeaux). The other was the edges and dispersal phase of marches. The calm main body of cortèges in mid-sized cities was lower-risk. CREDIBLE. No source breaks injuries down by declared union block versus cortège de tête (the front section where autonomous and black-bloc groups march). UNKNOWN.

## 4. New facts since 18 Oct
- **Justice:** VERIFIED. In Paris, 855 people were arrested (interpellations) and 557 held in garde à vue (police custody), 278 of them extended. The justice minister says 123 are now jailed, either on remand or under a mandat de dépôt (sent straight to prison after sentencing). He describes the detainees as "majoritairement masculins et adultes", with "profils très variés". Reported cases are mostly young men with no political profile; one 40-year-old is described as ultragauche (https://www.actu-juridique.fr/penal/emeutes-du-17-octobre-a-paris-les-mandats-de-depots-pleuvent-en-comparutions-immediates/ ; https://www.trtfrancais.com/article/8202c1130a58).
- **"Ultragauche" attribution:** still UNPROVEN. The Préfecture de police says "notamment" ultragauche, while the prosecutor and the case profiles point to a mixed population.
- **Infiltrators/provocateurs:** no new evidence. The claim remains ALLEGED (https://lemediaen442.fr/17-octobre-casseurs-et-policiers-les-images-qui-derangent/). The Limoges "police looting" story remains CONTRADICTED, and a complaint for false denunciation has been opened (franceinfo vrai-ou-fake _8243950).
- **IGPN (police internal inspectorate):** VERIFIED but no change. There are still 4 investigations for 17–18 Oct and 36 for the movement (https://www.franceinfo.fr/societe/mobilisation-du-17-octobre/violences-du-17-octobre-quatre-enquetes-ouvertes-par-l-inspection-generale-de-la-police-nationale_8244109.html).

## 5. Corrections to the prior briefs
1. "No government rebuttal found": **wrong**. The ministry denied the report to Mediapart on 19 Oct.
2. "One death": **possibly two**. The Paris parquet reportedly mentioned a second man found dead at Châtelet on the night of 18–19 Oct (Le Parisien, via https://lareleveetlapeste.fr/manifestations-du-17-octobre-2-morts-un-tournant/). Circumstances and any link to the protests are UNKNOWN, so the "uncorroborated militant claim" label should become "reported, unexplained".
3. Arrest figures: 1,200 was the early count on the Saturday night. The consolidated figure is 2,397 (https://www.ici.fr/infos/faits-divers-justice/mobilisation-du-17-octobre-1-800-interpellations-en-france-indique-le-ministre-de-l-interieur-laurent-nunez-6459330). The Paris figure of 557 counts gardes à vue, not arrests (855).
4. Bordeaux injury figures differ between the provisional and later counts. Treat all city tallies as provisional.
5. The risk ladder's claim that "17 Oct looting clustered in evening/wildcat actions" is **too reassuring**. Big-city gathering points in the early afternoon were also hot spots.
