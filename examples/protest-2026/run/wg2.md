## 1. Budget calendar (PLF 2027 = the 2027 finance bill)
- Assemblée (lower house) timetable: debate on part 1 (revenue) ends with the solemn vote **today, Tue 20 Oct**. Part 2 (spending, debated mission by mission) runs **27 Oct–16 Nov**. The vote on the whole bill is **Tue 17 Nov**. The Sénat and a CMP (joint committee of both houses) follow. The constitutional 70-day limit runs out around 10–18 Dec, depending on the outlet, after which the government can enact the budget by ordinance. **[CREDIBLE]** https://www.franceinfo.fr/politique/parlement-francais/assemblee-nationale/les-dates-cles-du-calendrier-parlementaire-pour-le-projet-de-loi-de-finance-2027_8159534.html ; https://www.legiwatch.fr/calendrier-plf-interactif/
- **"Enseignement scolaire" mission (state school funding, ~€65.5bn, −1,588 tenured teacher posts):** the exact day it is debated in the Assemblée chamber is **UNKNOWN**. No source links "12 Nov" to this mission. "Between 27 Oct and 16 Nov" is **CREDIBLE**. https://www.assemblee-nationale.fr/dyn/contenu/visualisation/1119828/file/organisation%20PLF%202027.pdf ; https://nosparlementaires.fr/actualites/postes-enseignants-suppressions-commissions-contre-49-3-budget-2027
- **Condition (new):** under the budget framework law (LOLF), part 2 cannot be debated until part 1 has been adopted. If today's vote rejects part 1, the whole bill goes to the Sénat and the Assemblée never debates the education mission at all. **[CREDIBLE: an established procedural rule; today's result is UNKNOWN]**
- **Article 49.3 / censure:** article 49.3 lets the government pass a text without a vote unless a censure motion brings it down. In Jan–Feb 2026 Lecornu used it on the 2026 budget, and the left's censure motions got about 260 votes against the 289 needed **[VERIFIED]** (https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/2/2/france-adopts-2026-budget-after-two-no-confidence-votes-fail). For 2027, all four left-wing groups have pledged censure **[CREDIBLE]**. The RN (about 122 seats) calls its stance a "position of openness": it will vote against the PS rejection motion and judge the text "sur pièces" (on its content), but does not rule out censure **[CREDIBLE]**. Lecornu keeps 49.3 and ordinances open. No 49.3 has been used on the 2027 bill yet. If 49.3 is used, the education funding total is set by the government's chosen text, not by a separate vote on the mission. https://www.boursorama.com/actualite-economique/actualites/budget-2027-lecornu-et-le-ps-se-confrontent-le-rn-veut-aller-au-debat-3afc929e37c6df00fab170696d0b0e8d ; https://www.franceinfo.fr/economie/budget/toutes-les-issues-restent-ouvertes-y-compris-la-voie-des-ordonnances-comment-sebastien-lecornu-veut-doter-la-france-d-un-budget-sans-etre-censure_8214587.html ; https://www.journaldunet.com/business/action-publique/1555095-la-gauche-annonce-une-motion-de-censure-contre-le-gouvernement-lecornu-lors-du-debat-sur-le-budget-2027/

**(b) Gilets jaunes.** A coordination formed after 17 Oct announced on 19 Oct actions on **Sat 24 Oct** and **Sat 31 Oct**, then joining 5 Nov **[VERIFIED: franceinfo, LyonMag, Sortiraparis]**. Labels vary: the organisers say "Acte II", and Le Figaro calls 31 Oct "Acte III". The organisers' map listed 124 actions, including Paris (Bastille, République) and Lyon (Bellecour, 14:00). Map entries do not guarantee an event will actually happen. **Paris:** a Préfecture de police order of 19 Oct sets a 23:00–06:00 curfew and bans undeclared gatherings **in certain districts**. Whether 24 Oct is declared or banned is not yet decided **[UNKNOWN as of 20 Oct]**. Demands: fuel prices and the cost of living. There is no formal platform. Le Figaro (a right-leaning paper) describes "troubled origins" **[ALLEGED: one outlet]**. https://www.franceinfo.fr/societe/mobilisation-du-17-octobre/mobilisation-du-17-octobre-une-coordination-de-gilets-jaunes-annonce-un-acte-ii-des-manifestations-pour-samedi-24-octobre_8243932.html ; https://www.sortiraparis.com/en/news/in-paris/articles/355062-curfew-in-paris-and-a-ban-on-demonstrations-in-the-affected-districts ; https://www.lyonmag.com/article/154471/gilets-jaunes-un-nouvel-appel-a-manifester-le-24-octobre-ils-reviendront-place-bellecour
