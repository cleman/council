**Expansionist:** Agreed. I'm dropping my "late October counts more than December" line for (c). The (c) window runs into December, so 5 Nov is the clearest single date, not the only one.

  - **(a) Lycéens:** the window is Lecornu's end-of-October decisions (date UNKNOWN), and it falls during the holidays. The Assemblée mission debate is CONDITIONAL on Part 1 passing and on no 49.3.
  - **(b) Gilets jaunes:** the weakest identifiable leverage path.
  - **(d) 17 Oct policing grievance:** the inquiry (VERIFIED) is a better channel than a march.
- There is no French evidence on the marginal effect of either marching or contacting MPs. All the cited studies are from the US.
- All advice is conditional on which demands the person shares. Abstaining is legitimate, especially for non-EU permit holders or anyone who shares none of the demands.

- Outsider's "1–404" claim is withdrawn pending a check.
