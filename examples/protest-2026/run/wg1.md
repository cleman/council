# WG1 FINAL: Events, policing and narratives (verified 20 Oct 2026)
Method: the researcher ran 14 WebSearch queries, and the verifier ran 10 more in extended mode. WebFetch does not work here, so every finding rests on search-engine summaries, not on full articles. Mediapart's own article was never read directly.

## 1. Mediapart, 19 Oct: "On nous demande d'écrire que LFI est derrière le mouvement" ("we are being asked to write that LFI is behind the movement")
- **Article exists and says what is reported:** VERIFIED. A commissioner in the RT (renseignement territorial, police intelligence) says in written exchanges dated 18 Oct that he was asked to write that LFI was behind the unrest, and calls this "objectivement faux" ("objectively false"). Sources: [France 24 press review](https://www.france24.com/fr/émissions/dans-la-presse/20261020-révélations-mediapart-la-fabrique-de-lfi-comme-bouc-émissaire-du-17-octobre), [Imaz Press](https://imazpress.com/selection-de-la-redaction/accusations-lfi).
- **The Interior Ministry denied it:** VERIFIED. Several outlets repeat that, asked by Mediapart, the ministry "a démenti avoir donné de telles consignes" ("denied giving any such instructions"). The denial appears inside Mediapart's article. There was no separate press release. ([Al Manar](https://french.almanar.com.lb/article/147972/), [Les mots ont un sens](https://www.lesmotsontunsens.com/on-nous-demande-decrire-que-cest-lfi-les-confidences-des-services-apres-le-fiasco-du-17-octobre/))
- **The government ordered a false report:** ALLEGED. It rests on one anonymous source, and the ministry denies it. No other outlet has checked it independently. Every other outlet found only repeats Mediapart.
- **Counter-detail:** a national police-union official quoted by Mediapart estimates that about 20% of the perpetrators were ultragauche (far left) and the rest were looters with no political label. This cuts against both narratives. It is CREDIBLE as a reported view and ALLEGED as a fact.
- **Follow-ups:** VERIFIED. Bompard (LFI) will refer Nuñez to the CJR (Cour de justice de la République, the court that tries ministers) ([franceinfo](https://www.franceinfo.fr/politique/la-france-insoumise/le-depute-lfi-manuel-bompard-denonce-un-scandale-d-etat-et-va-saisir-la-cour-de-justice-de-la-republique-contre-le-ministre-de-l-interieur_8245195.html)).

## 2. Assemblée commission of inquiry
VERIFIED. On 20 Oct, the conférence des présidents (the Assemblée's steering body) approved, with every group in favour, Yaël Braun-Pivet's proposal to give the commission des lois (law committee) the powers of an inquiry. It covers the violence of 17 Oct, violent groups, and the strategy of maintien de l'ordre (crowd policing). It can compel witnesses, demand documents and take testimony under oath, and its target is to report by the end of the year. Who will chair it and how many rapporteurs it will have remain UNKNOWN. LFI and the left object to a Renaissance chair. ([LCP](https://lcp.fr/actualites/info-lcp-17-octobre-l-assemblee-nationale-approuve-la-creation-de-la-commission-d), [franceinfo](https://www.franceinfo.fr/societe/mobilisation-du-17-octobre/creation-d-une-commission-d-enquete-confiee-a-la-commission-des-lois-de-l-assemblee-nationale-apres-les-violences-du-17-octobre_8245390.html))

## 3. Deaths: two, neither explained
- **Bd Magenta, Paris, 17 Oct:** VERIFIED. A protester collapsed and died. The Préfecture says he probably had a cardio-respiratory arrest and that no link to the violence has been established. MPs are demanding explanations, and no autopsy result has been published. ([CNews](https://www.cnews.fr/faits-divers/2026-10-18/mobilisation-du-17-octobre-un-manifestant-est-mort-paris-des-suites-dun), [Yahoo/AFP](https://fr.news.yahoo.com/exigeons-explications-mort-d-homme-073716695.html))
- **Rue Saint-Denis, Châtelet, night of 18–19 Oct:** CREDIBLE as a death, UNKNOWN as to cause. A 48-year-old man died there (Le Parisien, relayed by [L'Express/Orange](https://actu.orange.fr/france/paris-un-homme-meurt-dans-le-quartier-de-chatelet-en-marge-d-affrontements-avec-les-forces-de-l-ordre-l-express-via-6medias-CNT000002tb6J4.html)). A witness describes police charges around 00:20, blood at the back of his skull, and a crowd surge. A police source says he collapsed on his own. No prosecutor statement was found, and Le Parisien says no link to the violence has been established. Viral posts on X claiming "killed by a police charge" are ALLEGED.

## 4. Numbers
- **Official national figures, 18 Oct evening:** VERIFIED. 2,397 arrests and 997 police and gendarmes injured, 7 of them seriously. Earlier figures (1,200, then 1,800) were provisional. ([ICI](https://www.ici.fr/infos/faits-divers-justice/mobilisation-du-17-octobre-1-800-interpellations-en-france-indique-le-ministre-de-l-interieur-laurent-nunez-6459330))
- **Paris:** CREDIBLE. 855 arrests and 557 placed in garde à vue (GAV, police custody).
- **People jailed:** VERIFIED that the number is 123. **Correction:** this is a *national* figure, not Paris. It counts people on remand or given an immediate prison order (mandat de dépôt). About 50 judicial investigations are open. 69% of those involved are adults. GAV can now be extended to 72h under "organised crime" rules. National GAV totals conflict (2,331 vs 2,513). ([Europe 1](https://www.europe1.fr/politique/violences-urbaines-123-personnes-sont-incarcerees-ce-mardi-matin-souligne-gerald-darmanin-1166025), [TRT](https://www.trtfrancais.com/article/8202c1130a58))
- **Injured protesters:** UNKNOWN. No official, medic, LDH (Ligue des droits de l'Homme) or Observatoire count exists. Local préfecture figures, such as Rennes 27 and Bordeaux 8, are CREDIBLE but provisional. Street medics treated about ten people at République alone, some of them hurt by désencerclement (stun) grenades ([Politis](https://www.politis.fr/articles/2026/10/manifestations-du-17-octobre-cest-la-convergence-des-coleres-aujourdhui/)). Serious injuries are VERIFIED: a hand torn off in Saint-Étienne, an eye lost in Mulhouse, a head wound in Paris.

## 5. When and where violence happened
- **Paris, République:** CREDIBLE. Clashes started around midday, and from 13:00 the Préfecture reported "several hundred violent people" ([Europe 1 live](https://www.europe1.fr/societe/en-direct-mobilisation-du-17-octobre-breve-intrusion-de-manifestants-dans-la-mairie-de-lille-scenes-de-chaos-a-bordeaux-et-paris-1156250), [CNews](https://www.cnews.fr/faits-divers/2026-10-17/mobilisation-du-17-octobre-le-socle-de-la-statue-de-la-place-de-la)). Looting continued that afternoon and the next night, including Châtelet around midnight on 18–19 Oct.
- **Lille:** CREDIBLE. Crowd surges began before the march left at about 14:00–14:30, and a McDonald's burned on the Grand'Place ([ICI Lille](https://www.ici.fr/hauts-de-france/nord-59/lille/mobilisation-du-17-octobre-des-premieres-tensions-a-lille-entre-forces-de-l-ordre-et-manifestants-7548967)). "12:30" is NOT confirmed.
- **Lyon:** CREDIBLE. Tear gas was used at Bellecour from 14:00. In Bordeaux, the march was gassed at its assembly point around 14:00.
- **Pattern:** CREDIBLE. Risk concentrated in two places: assembly points in big cities from early afternoon, and the edges, dispersal and night of the marches. The calm main body of marches in mid-sized cities carried less risk.
- **New, relevant to anyone deciding:** CREDIBLE. Localised curfews and bans now apply. Bordeaux hypercentre has a curfew for minors from 22:00 to 07:00, 20–26 Oct ([ICI](https://www.ici.fr/nouvelle-aquitaine/gironde-33/bordeaux/violences-du-17-octobre-un-couvre-feu-instaure-a-bordeaux-a-partir-de-ce-mardi-6094204)). Lyon had a ban on gatherings from 18:00 to 21:00 plus a night curfew. Place de la République in Paris was under curfew overnight 19–20 Oct, and the Nord has curfews for unaccompanied minors ([i24](https://www.i24news.tv/fr/actu/france/artc-france-des-couvre-feux-instaures-dans-plusieurs-villes-apres-les-violences)). Check your préfecture's arrêtés (local orders) before going.

2. The 123 jailed are national, not Paris. The draft had misattributed the figure.
3. Lille clashes "from about 12:30" are downgraded. Sources say about 14:00, before the march left. Paris is corrected to midday/13:00.
4. The Châtelet death now has a location (rue Saint-Denis), a time and the two conflicting accounts. It is graded CREDIBLE, and the reference to a "parquet" (prosecutor) statement is removed because none was found. The draft relied on a militant outlet. The mainstream relay through L'Express and Le Parisien supports the death but not any cause.
5. Added the union official's "~20% ultragauche" estimate as counter-evidence against both the "LFI" and the "pure looters" narratives.
6. Added curfews and bans. The draft missed them, and they directly affect minors and evening attendance.
7. Dropped the uncorroborated line about Nuñez calling the affair "récupération politique".
