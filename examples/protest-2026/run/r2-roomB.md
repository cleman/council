# Round 2, Room B: Contrarian, First Principles, Local Realist, Outsider

## Debate
**Outsider:** FD3 settles it: 2,397 arrests is the latest official provisional figure (VERIFIED). Against 550,000 marchers (Interior count) that gives 0.44% nationally, 0.78% in Paris and 0.11% in Strasbourg (CREDIBLE). These rates are **upper bounds**, because the arrests include looters who never marched (Toulouse 1,500–2,000; Grenoble ~400) and union turnout counts are 2–14× higher. The real rate for the peaceful main body is UNKNOWN but lower.
**Contrarian:** "Lower" is not "low". Protester injuries are uncounted (UNKNOWN), but a hand torn off and an eye lost are VERIFIED. On 13 Oct the youth sections of a declared march were gassed (CREDIBLE). Lyon is CONTRADICTED; exclude it.
**First Principles:** So rank cells by probability × consequence. An arrest without charge means a TAJ police-file entry for a citizen (VERIFIED), but it can mean a permit review for a non-EU resident (VERIFIED law; frequency UNKNOWN). Off-street actions and the strike carry no arrest exposure: "go" for all.
**Local Realist:** Place and time count too. Risk peaked at big-city assembly points from 12:00–14:00, then at edges, dispersal and night (CREDIBLE). "Evening" means avoid, for everyone.
**Contrarian:** Non-EU permit holders should avoid even the union march: the loss is irreversible. **Local Realist:** Caution. The case law treats an isolated act as possibly insufficient (CREDIBLE). **First Principles:** Record the cell as split.
**Outsider:** Minors at a daytime lycéen march in a city with no curfew: caution. Hotspot or near curfew: avoid (~200+ pupils injured, counts CONTRADICTED).
**Contrarian:** Undeclared 24 Oct gatherings sit at the same hotspots: avoid. **Local Realist:** Undeclared is not banned: caution for adult citizens.

- **(3):** arrests ran at ≤0.44%, lower in the main body (CREDIBLE upper bound). An arrest creates a TAJ entry that is not deleted automatically (VERIFIED). †A non-EU permit can be reviewed without a conviction (VERIFIED law; frequency UNKNOWN). - **(4):** youth sections were gassed on 13 Oct (CREDIBLE). ‡Only in daytime, with no curfew in force, and away from the front.
- **(5):** taking part in a banned gathering is an offence. Risk concentrates at evening and dispersal, and both deaths are unexplained. *Contested. Public servants: no badge or official-role statements, under the duty of restraint (devoir de réserve) (CREDIBLE).

**Checklist:** check the préfecture's orders (arrêtés) the night before and that morning. Stay in the stewarded middle, away from the front, youth sections and edges. Leave before dispersal and before dark. Buddy up; set a meeting point. Bring water and ID; leave your phone or bring a spare. Write a lawyer's number on your arm. Sign no search consent without a lawyer; refusing to give your unlock code may be an offence (VERIFIED).
**Kettling plan:** stay grouped; ask officers for the exit point, which the doctrine requires "as soon as circumstances allow" (VERIFIED), and leave through it. Note the time, place and officers' ID numbers, then report to the LDH (human rights league) or the IGPN (police internal affairs).
