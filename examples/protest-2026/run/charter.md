## Quality rules (from the assessment — binding)
1. **Genuine neutrality on the cause.** Do not recommend substantive policy positions (e.g. "ask your MP to restore X posts") as if they were neutral. Advice must be conditional on which demands the person actually shares. Distinguish clearly: (a) the lycéen movement (schools), (b) Gilets jaunes "Acte II" (cost of living/fuel), (c) intersyndicale 5 Nov strike (wages/public services/budget), (d) the person's possible grievance about 17 Oct policing/narratives.
2. **Abstention is a legitimate option** and must be argued honestly, not as a strawman.
3. **Resolve factual conflicts, don't wave them away.** Known open issues: exact date the "Enseignement scolaire" mission is examined in the Assemblée (12 Nov vs window 27 Oct–16 Nov); whether/when the Interior Ministry denied Mediapart's 19 Oct report (prior brief said no response found, a later advisor cited an AA article saying it denied); whether the Bordeaux route link is from 13 Oct not 5 Nov; whether lycéen marches exist during Toussaint (Lyon 20 & 27 Oct found).
4. **Precise categories.** EU citizens vs non-EU residents face different removal rules — be exact.
5. **Complete risk picture.** Arrest rate is not the only risk: protester injuries (no official count — find observer/medic counts), and concentration of risk by place and time of day.
6. **Reader-friendly.** English, plain language, define acronyms (GAV, LBD, OQTF, TAJ, sommations, nasse, intersyndicale, IGPN, PLF…), no repetition.
7. **Grade evidence:** VERIFIED / CREDIBLE / ALLEGED / CONTRADICTED / UNKNOWN, with URLs. WebFetch is broken here; rely on WebSearch summaries and say so.
