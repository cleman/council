# Opening position — Local Realist

## 1. Position
There is no national answer. A daytime 5 Nov union march in a mid-sized city, a Saturday 24 Oct Gilets jaunes gathering at République or Bellecour that runs into the evening, and a 15-year-old's holiday lycéen march are three different decisions. If you share a strand's demands, join it **only in the form your city, the hour and your legal status make tolerable**: daytime, the main body of the march, a declared route, and leave before it disperses. Abstaining, or taking a low-exposure route, is the rational choice in these cases:
- your local event is banned or under curfew;
- you hold a non-EU residence permit;
- you are a minor in a big-city hotspot.

## 2. Arguments
- **Risk clusters in place and time.** On 17 Oct, trouble began at big-city assembly points in the early afternoon (Paris République from about 12:00–13:00, Lyon and Bordeaux from about 14:00). It then moved to edges, dispersals and the night, when the Châtelet death happened around 00:20. Calm marches in mid-sized cities carried less risk [WG1 §5 CREDIBLE]. Paris alone had 855 of the 2,397 national arrests [CREDIBLE/VERIFIED].
- **The legal ground is local and changes daily.** Curfews or bans are in force in Paris, Bordeaux (for minors), Lyon Presqu'île and the Nord [WG1 §5, WG3 §5 CREDIBLE]. As of 19–20 Oct, Paris, Lyon and Dijon had not yet decided about 24 Oct [WG2 UNKNOWN; my search: [dijon-actualites](https://dijon-actualites.fr/2026/10/19/dijon-la-manifestation-des-gilets-jaunes-du-24-octobre-sera-t-elle-interdite-nathalie-koenders-va-sentretenir-avec-la-prefete/), [Tribune de Lyon](https://tribunedelyon.fr/societe/gilets-jaunes-ce-qui-est-prevu-24-octobre-lyon/)]. Joining a banned gathering is itself an offence. Check your préfecture's arrêtés (local orders) the evening before and again that morning.
- **Your profile changes the stakes.**
  - Non-EU permit holder: an arrest can lead to a permit review [WG3 §1 VERIFIED law].
  - EU citizen: removal risk is near-negligible.
  - Minors: curfews apply to them, and about 200 pupils have been injured [WG3 §6, figures CONTRADICTED].
  - Security, airport or justice jobs: the TAJ police-file entry matters [WG3 §2].

2. What were the arrest and injury counts per city outside Paris on 17 Oct? These would test whether mid-sized cities really were lower-risk.
