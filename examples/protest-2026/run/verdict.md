# Should I join the autumn 2026 protests? Council 2 verdict (20 Oct 2026)

Grades: VERIFIED, CREDIBLE, ALLEGED (single or interested source), CONTRADICTED, UNKNOWN. Based on search summaries only (section 11).

## 1. Bottom line

There is no single "movement" but four strands with different demands and risks. The answer depends on whether you share a strand's demands and what an arrest would cost you. The council takes no side on any demand. If you share none, or an arrest would cost you dearly, staying home or acting off the street is sound. If you share one and your risk is low, the lowest-risk street option is a declared, daytime march: walk in the main body and leave before it disperses.

## 3. The case for not participating

Abstainer's paragraph (accepted in Round 3), updated:

> Staying home is a reasoned choice. 17 Oct brought 2,397 arrests (VERIFIED, provisional), 123 people jailed, a hand torn off and an eye lost (VERIFIED). Injured protesters were never counted (UNKNOWN). Kettling is lawful, so you only partly control your exposure. An arrest without charge still leaves a TAJ police-file entry (VERIFIED). For a non-EU resident it can trigger a permit review (VERIFIED law; how often is UNKNOWN). No French evidence measures what one extra marcher changes, or one extra email to an MP either (UNKNOWN). The budget turns on votes, 49.3 and censure arithmetic (CREDIBLE). Your absence costs the cause little; your arrest could cost you a lot. It needs no justification beyond your priorities and says nothing about the demands' merits.

This applies even to low-risk people who share a demand. Do not base the choice on either unproven 17 Oct claim (LFI orchestration, or a manufactured scapegoat): see section 9.

## 4. The case for participating

- **The window for (c) is real.** The budget stays open until about mid-December by any path (CREDIBLE), and 5 Nov falls before the 17 Nov vote if Part 1 passes (section 5). A strike has an economic cost that a petition lacks.
- **The government has responded in part:** about 3,000 replacement teachers (redeployed TZR, not new hires, CREDIBLE) and +€1.2bn for education (VERIFIED as announcements), with more decisions promised by end-October (VERIFIED; date UNKNOWN).
- **Peaceful turnout has measured effects, in the US** (Madestam et al. 2013; Wasow 2020; VERIFIED as published). No French study exists, so the council gives no figure per marcher.
- **Risk is partly manageable:** on 17 Oct the stewarded main body was calmer than assembly points and edges (CREDIBLE).

## 7. Where and when risk concentrates, plus a safety and kettling plan

On 17 Oct, trouble began at big-city assembly points around 12:00–14:00, then the front, youth sections, edges, dispersal and night. City size did not predict it: Grenoble, Rennes and Brest saw serious disorder (both CREDIBLE).

**Legal status** (VERIFIED: [Cour de cassation 21-81.054](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/juri/id/JURITEXT000045939979), [justice.fr](https://www.justice.fr/themes/manifestations-illicites)):
- **Undeclared:** joining is no offence; only organisers are liable.
- **Banned** (by an advance arrêté préfectoral): a 4th-class fine: €135 standard, €375 increased, up to €750 if a court decides (VERIFIED); one activist guide's €1,500 is wrong (CONTRADICTED).
- **After the sommations:** staying on purpose is a criminal offence (*délit*): up to 1 year and €15,000; 3 years and €45,000 if masked.
- **Face covering** in or beside a demonstration, to avoid identification, is itself an offence (art. 431-9-1, 1 year/€15,000, VERIFIED); a medical mask against gas is a grey zone.

**Checklist**
- [ ] Check the arrêtés the evening before and that morning. Bring a buddy, water, ID and a lawyer's number on your arm. Don't cover your face.
- [ ] Join after set-off, stay in the stewarded middle, leave before dispersal, dark and curfew.
- [ ] Phone: leave it home or bring a spare. Refusing your unlock code can be an offence (VERIFIED). Sign no search consent without a lawyer (CREDIBLE).
- [ ] TAJ: an arrest creates an entry even without charge, not deleted automatically; ask the prosecutor to delete it (VERIFIED).
- [ ] 5 Nov: rail unions strike (VERIFIED); plan getting home without SNCF trains, before dark.
- [ ] Kettling: police must open an exit "as soon as circumstances allow" (VERIFIED). Stay grouped, ask for it, note time, place and officers' ID numbers.
- [ ] Complaints (VERIFIED, combinable): the [Défenseur des droits form](https://formulaire.defenseurdesdroits.fr) (does not pause legal deadlines); a named [IGPN report](https://www.police-nationale.interieur.gouv.fr/Organisation/Inspection-Generale-de-la-Police-Nationale/Signalement-IGPN) (URL unchecked), or IGGN for gendarmes; a criminal complaint to police or the procureur, the only route to prosecution. The LDH can help.

## 12. Glossary

- **49.3:** passes a text without a vote unless censure succeeds.
- **ASSD / GMD:** a launched stun grenade / a hand-thrown dispersal grenade.
- **Arrêté préfectoral:** a local State order (ban, curfew).
- **Censure:** vote to topple the government.
- **CESEDA:** foreigners' residence code.
- **Défenseur des droits:** rights ombudsman.
- **Devoir de réserve:** duty of restraint.
- **IGPN / IGGN:** police / gendarmerie inspectorates.
- **Intersyndicale:** joint union front.
- **LDH:** human-rights league.
- **LFI:** a left party.
- **Lycéen:** high-school pupil.
- **La Cimade / GISTI:** migrants' rights groups.
- **Mission:** budget chapter.
- **Nasse:** kettling (police encircle a crowd).
- **Parcoursup:** university-admissions platform.
- **Part 1 (of the PLF):** the revenue half; if it is rejected, the whole bill falls.
- **PLF:** budget bill.
- **Préfecture / préfet:** the State's local office / representative.
- **Procureur:** prosecutor.
- **RT:** police intelligence.
- **SNCF:** national railway.
- **Sommations:** formal orders to disperse.
- **TAJ:** police records file.
- **TZR:** roaming substitute teacher.
