# Opening position — Contrarian

## 1. Position
The question assumes there is one "movement" to join. There is not, and every option has a serious flaw, including staying home. My verdict: **do not join "the movement" as a block.** If you share the demands of one strand, choose that strand's **declared, daytime, union-run event**. In practice that is the 5 Nov intersyndicale march, and you stay in its main body. Skip the 24 and 31 Oct Gilets jaunes dates in big-city centres. If you share none of the demands, staying home is fully legitimate. But stay home because of your own priorities, not because of either side's story about 17 Oct.

## 2. Arguments
**a) The 24 and 31 Oct dates combine the highest risk with the vaguest purpose.** The Gilets jaunes coordination has no formal platform, its map entries are not guaranteed, and the Paris status for 24 Oct is UNKNOWN. A curfew and partial bans are in force (WG2 §2b, VERIFIED/UNKNOWN). On 17 Oct the risk was concentrated at big-city assembly points from early afternoon and at night (WG1 §5, CREDIBLE). Two people died and neither death is explained (WG1 §3). Protester injuries are UNKNOWN in number, but they include a hand torn off and an eye lost (VERIFIED). Kettling is lawful and current (WG3 §4, VERIFIED).

**b) Marching for schools may not reach the lever you think it reaches.** The Assemblée debates the "Enseignement scolaire" mission only if Part 1 passed today's vote. I could not find the result; it is UNKNOWN. If the government uses 49.3, there is no separate vote on that mission (WG2 §1, CREDIBLE). The 12 Nov date for that debate is unsupported. Anyone protesting to influence a specific vote should know it may never happen.

**c) Staying home has its own flaw: the narratives are weaker than they look.** "LFI is behind it" rests on a disputed claim that the RT itself calls "objectively false." Its opposite, "the government fabricated the report," is ALLEGED, from one anonymous source, and the ministry denied it (VERIFIED). A police-union estimate of about 20% ultragauche cuts against both stories (WG1 §1). If you abstain because of either narrative, you are abstaining on evidence that has not been tested.

**Profile veto:** if you hold a temporary or multi-year non-EU permit, or you are a minor, a single arrest can follow you through the TAJ police file and the prefecture's checks (WG3 §1–2, VERIFIED law). For you, even the "safe" option is not low-stakes.
