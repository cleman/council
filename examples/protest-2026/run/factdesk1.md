## 1. PLF 2027: Part 1 vote, PS motion de rejet, 49.3, censure

- **Correction to the brief: the PS motion de rejet has already been voted on.** It was defeated on **13 Oct**, 149 for and 236 against. Horizons and LIOT voted against it. The PS kept the motion despite a letter from Lecornu asking it to withdraw. **VERIFIED.** [franceinfo](https://www.franceinfo.fr/economie/budget/direct-budget-2027-le-projet-de-loi-de-finances-arrive-dans-l-hemicycle-mardi-en-fin-de-journee_8235388.html), [LCP](https://lcp.fr/actualites/il-laisse-la-porte-fermee-les-socialistes-repondent-a-sebastien-lecornu-et-maintiennent), [NosParlementaires](https://nosparlementaires.fr/actualites/budget-2027-calendrier-assemblee-cinq-semaines)
- **Today's vote on Part 1 (recettes): no result published yet. UNKNOWN.** The solemn vote is set for **Tuesday 20 Oct, after questions to the government (QAG)**. The vote on the whole bill is set for **17 Nov**. **CREDIBLE** ([NosParlementaires](https://nosparlementaires.fr/actualites/budget-2027-calendrier-assemblee-cinq-semaines), [Legifiscal](https://www.legifiscal.fr/actualites-fiscales/4684-plf-2027-commission-finances-rejette-partie-recettes-creuse-deficit-environ-10-md.html)).
  - The finance committee rejected Part 1 on 9 Oct (31 against, 3 for), but the floor debates the government's text. **VERIFIED** ([LCP](https://lcp.fr/actualites/budget-2027-les-deputes-rejettent-en-commission-la-partie-recettes-du-projet-de-loi-de)).
  - After four days on the floor, only 7 of the 58 articles had been examined, with more than 2,800 amendments still pending. Part of the text may therefore never have been debated before the vote. **CREDIBLE.**
- **What follows from each outcome** (standard procedure, **CREDIBLE**):
  - **Part 1 rejected:** the whole PLF counts as rejected in the Assemblée. It goes to the Sénat with no debates on the missions in the Assemblée. The "Enseignement scolaire" mission would not be debated there at first reading. This happened with the PLF 2025 in Nov 2024.
  - **Part 1 adopted:** the missions are debated from about 27 Oct to 16 Nov, and the whole bill is voted on 17 Nov.
  - **49.3 used** (on Part 1 now, or later): there is no separate vote on the missions, and a motion de censure is likely.
- **Government signals on 49.3:**
  - On 17 Sep, Lecornu promised "neither 49.3 nor ordinances" on condition that there is no parliamentary obstruction. **VERIFIED** ([France 24](https://www.france24.com/fr/france/20260917-lecornu-pr%C3%A9sente-un-budget-offensif-avec-54-milliards-d-%C3%A9conomies)).
  - Since then, franceinfo reports the line "all options remain open, including the ordinance route". Ordinances could be used from about 18 Dec, after 70 days. **CREDIBLE** ([franceinfo](https://www.franceinfo.fr/economie/budget/toutes-les-issues-restent-ouvertes-y-compris-la-voie-des-ordonnances-comment-sebastien-lecornu-veut-doter-la-france-d-un-budget-sans-etre-censure_8214587.html)).
  - No 49.3 announcement was found for today. **UNKNOWN.**
- **Censure:**
  - The PS is keeping a threat of censure if it gets no concessions (letter of 2 Oct). **CREDIBLE** ([Echos Plus](https://echosplus.com/2026/10/03/budget-2027-le-ps-presse-lecornu-de-revoir-sa-copie-et-maintient-la-menace-de-censure/)).
  - The RN said on 13 Oct that it wanted to "go to the debate". It did not back the PS motion de rejet. **CREDIBLE** ([Boursorama/AFP](https://www.boursorama.com/actualite-economique/actualites/budget-2027-lecornu-et-le-ps-se-confrontent-le-rn-veut-aller-au-debat-3afc929e37c6df00fab170696d0b0e8d)).

## 2. Assemblée commission of inquiry into 17 Oct

- **Created in principle today (20 Oct).** All groups approved it at the Conférence des présidents. The commission des Lois will take on the powers of a commission of inquiry. Its remit covers the violence and looting and the policing strategy (maintien de l'ordre). Yaël Braun-Pivet wants conclusions by the end of 2026. **VERIFIED** ([LCP](https://lcp.fr/actualites/info-lcp-17-octobre-l-assemblee-nationale-approuve-la-creation-de-la-commission-d), [franceinfo](https://www.franceinfo.fr/societe/mobilisation-du-17-octobre/creation-d-une-commission-d-enquete-confiee-a-la-commission-des-lois-de-l-assemblee-nationale-apres-les-violences-du-17-octobre_8245390.html), [Europe 1](https://www.europe1.fr/politique/17-octobre-yael-braun-pivet-va-proposer-une-commission-denquete-parlementaire-sur-les-violences-et-la-strategie-de-maintien-de-l-ordre-1160313)).
- **Chair:** the commission des Lois is chaired by Renaissance, so a Renaissance chair is expected. The left, including LFI, fears the inquiry will be used for political ends. The number of rapporteurs and which group gets them are still open. Florent Boudié could not be confirmed as chair for this inquiry. **CREDIBLE for a Renaissance chair; named chair UNKNOWN.**
- **Public testimony or evidence submissions:** no call for testimony, online form or email channel has been announced. **UNKNOWN.**
  - In general, such commissions hear witnesses under oath, and the public can write to the commission through the Assemblée website or by post. Expect the commission's page to appear on assemblee-nationale.fr once it is set up.
  - A separate Senate inquiry has been requested by the Ecologist senators (Benarroche). The RN in the Senate opposes it. **CREDIBLE** ([Public Sénat](https://www.publicsenat.fr/actualites/politique/violences-du-17-octobre-au-senat-lheure-des-comptes-pour-le-gouvernement)).

## 3. Lecornu's "end of October" education decisions

- In his 7 Oct address, Lecornu promised the "first decisions" by the end of October. He set five priority areas: teaching conditions, school time, school buildings, Parcoursup and lycée democracy. He cited 40,000 contributions to the student consultation platform. **VERIFIED** ([franceinfo](https://www.franceinfo.fr/politique/gouvernement-de-sebastien-lecornu/lycees-gazole-electricite-budget-ce-qu-il-faut-retenir-de-l-allocution-du-premier-ministre-sebastien-lecornu_8227297.html), [L'Etudiant](https://www.letudiant.fr/lycee/mouvement-lyceen-alors-que-la-mobilisation-continue-le-gouvernement-lance-cinq-chantiers-prioritaires.html)).
- **No exact date has been announced. UNKNOWN.** The deadline falls during the Toussaint school holidays, and the date should be checked again around 26–31 Oct.
