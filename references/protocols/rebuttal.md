# Protocol: rebuttal round (Standard level)

After the openings, each seat reads the others and responds once. This is where most of the real debate happens at Standard level: in testing, the rebuttal round surfaced the decisive timing argument and corrected two factual errors.

Resume each seat's agent (so it keeps its context) or start a new one with its opening attached.

```
Rebuttal round: read the other seats' openings: <list of files>.
Still as the <Seat>, in 150–250 words:
- name the strongest argument against your own position;
- attack the weakest claim made by another seat (by name), with evidence;
- say whether you change your position, and why.
You may run up to 2 web searches to check a disputed point.
Save to <run>/rebuttal-<slug>.md and return it.
```

Point the seats at the main tension you noticed in the openings ("pay attention to X's timing argument") so the round doesn't scatter.
